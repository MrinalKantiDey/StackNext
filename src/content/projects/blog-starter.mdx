## Challenges

Culturenomics™ was experiencing significant revenue leakage due to severe performance bottlenecks on their existing WordPress website. 

Slow page load times were causing high bounce rates, frustrating users, and directly suppressing conversion rates. They needed a technical overhaul to stabilize the platform without losing their existing content architecture.

## SEO — The Complete Stack

Most starters ship three meta tags and call it SEO. Yoast Plugin the complete implementation:

- **JSON-LD structured data** — `WebSite`, `Organization`, and `Person` schemas on the homepage; `BlogPosting` on every post — prerequisites for rich results in Google Search
- **Open Graph + Twitter Cards** — full social metadata on every page, with post cover images wired in automatically
- **Canonical URLs** — constructed from the production domain, correct across staging and preview environments
- **Auto-generated sitemap** — every page included at build time; submit once to Search Console
- **Per-page robots control** — `noindex` / `nofollow` on any page via a prop
- **Search Console verification** — Google and Bing codes via `Yoast plugin`, no template edits
