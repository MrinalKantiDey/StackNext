## The Brief

PawPro is a pet grooming service that used to operate entirely through phone calls. Customers had to call for appointments, and the team had to manage everything manually. This made the entire process slow, confusing, and difficult to track.
After launching the online platform, PawPro saw a rise of nearly 70% in customer inquiries. The convenience of online booking also increased confirmed appointments by about 65% within the first three months.

## Problem

The main issue was that their booking and management system was completely offline. Handling appointments through calls led to missed bookings, no proper scheduling, and a lack of organized customer data.

## What was Our Tech Stack?

PawPro’s digital system was built using reliable and scalable technologies that supported smooth performance across web and mobile. Each part of the solution was developed with tools that ensured speed, stability, and long-term growth.

- **Website Development** — React, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Online Booking System** — React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB
- **Management Dashboard** — React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB
- **Mobile App Development** — React Native
- **Hosting and Deployment** — AWS (Amazon Web Services)
- **SEO** — Breadcrumbs, structured data, Open Graph, canonical, sitemap, robots

## The Process

We started with basic research to understand how online pet grooming platforms usually work. Then we designed the UI/UX, created the user flow, and finally developed the full system end-to-end. 

We helped PawPro move their entire service online by building a complete digital solution:
- A clean and user-friendly **website**
- An **online booking system** for grooming services
- A **management dashboard** for the team to handle bookings and schedules

## Results

What Happened After Partnering with Stacknxt Inc?

After going online, PawPro saw a noticeable increase in leads. Booking management became easier for the team, and customers could book grooming services with just a few taps. Overall, the digital shift made their operations faster, more organized, and customer-friendly.

- **70%** increase in customer inquiries
- **65%** rise in confirmed bookings
- **90%** fewer booking-related errors
- Faster scheduling and better visibility
- More repeat customers are using the **mobile app**
