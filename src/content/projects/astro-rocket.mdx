## SEO — The Complete Stack

Most starters ship three meta tags and call it SEO. Astro Rocket ships the complete implementation:

- **JSON-LD structured data** — `WebSite`, `Organization`, and `Person` schemas on the homepage; `BlogPosting` on every post — prerequisites for rich results in Google Search
- **Open Graph + Twitter Cards** — full social metadata on every page, with post cover images wired in automatically
- **Canonical URLs** — constructed from the production domain, correct across staging and preview environments
- **Auto-generated sitemap** — every page included at build time; submit once to Search Console
- **Per-page robots control** — `noindex` / `nofollow` on any page via a prop
- **Search Console verification** — Google and Bing codes via `.env`, no template edits

The full setup — what each piece does, how to configure it, and what rich results it unlocks — is in [SEO in Astro Rocket](/blog/seo-in-astro-rocket).

## Solution

We shifted their entire workflow to a digital system by building:

- A complete management dashboard for the company
- A dedicated mobile app for clients, dealers, and employees

This helped automate appointments, track tasks, and organize booking and handover processes in a smooth and timely manner.

## Process

We began with basic research to understand how real estate builders manage their internal operations. Then we designed a simple and clear interface for every role — clients, staff, and dealers. After finalizing the design, we developed the full system and ensured all features worked seamlessly together.

## Result

The digital system significantly improved their speed and organization. Client appointments became easier to manage, team tasks were completed on time, and deals were closed faster.
Overall, the new solution made their entire workflow more efficient, transparent, and user-friendly.

**What Happened After Going Digital with Stacknxt Inc.?**

- Nearly 65% reduction in delays related to site visits and appointments
- Around 70% improvement in task completion timelines
- Almost 80% fewer booking and handover-related errors
- 55% Faster deal closures with better sales coordination
- Improved transparency across teams, dealers, and clients
